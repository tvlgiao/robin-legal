Robin Bedtime Stories — Support

Need help? Email contact@coworkgamestudio.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Restore my purchase

Open Settings → Purchases → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. All 35 stories unlock again.

Do I need the internet?

No. All stories and narration are stored on the device. The internet is only used to buy or restore the one-time unlock.

Sleep timer and lock screen

Tap the moon chip in the player to choose a sleep timer. Stories keep playing with the screen locked; use the lock-screen controls to pause.

Reading speed and text size

Tap the speed chip (1×) in the player to slow down or speed up the narrator. Text size is in Settings.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
