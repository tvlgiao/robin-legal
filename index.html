Privacy Policy — Robin Bedtime Stories

Last updated: 2026-06-03

Robin Bedtime Stories ("the app") is a calm reading companion for children aged 6 to 10. We built it with a simple promise: bedtime should be private. The app has no account, no sign-in, and no analytics. All stories and audio are bundled in the app and play fully offline. The only time the app talks to the internet is to process and restore an optional one-time in-app purchase (see "Purchases" below).

What we collect

No personal information. The app does not collect, transmit, or upload your name, email, contacts, location, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or telemetry. There is no tracking and no advertising.

The only data that leaves the device is the in-app purchase transaction described under "Purchases", and only if you choose to buy or restore the premium unlock.

What we store locally on your device

To remember your preferences between sessions, the app saves the following settings in the operating system's local preferences storage. This data never leaves the device.

Stored valuePurpose

You can erase all of this instantly by deleting the app — there is no cloud copy.

Purchases

The app offers a single optional one-time purchase that unlocks all stories ("premium unlock"). It is not a subscription. Purchases are protected behind a parental gate.

  • The purchase itself is handled by Apple's App Store (or Google Play on Android). Your payment details are managed entirely by Apple/Google — we never see or store them.
  • To unlock content and let you restore your purchase on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, a third-party purchase-management service. When you buy or restore, the store purchase receipt and an anonymous app-user identifier generated by RevenueCat are processed to confirm your entitlement. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any contact information.
  • This is the only feature that connects to the internet. If you never purchase or restore, the app makes no network calls.

RevenueCat acts as our purchase processor. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

What we share

We do not share data with advertising networks, analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or marketing partners. The app contains no advertising or third-party tracking code. Purchase information is shared only with Apple/Google and RevenueCat for the sole purpose of processing and restoring your purchase, as described above.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children aged 6 to 10 and is built to comply with Apple's and Google's kid-directed guidelines:

  • No Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) is requested or used.
  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs are bundled.
  • No user-generated content, no chat, no social features.
  • The optional in-app purchase is placed behind a parental gate (a simple math question) so it cannot be triggered by a child unintentionally.
  • The age entered during onboarding is optional and used only on the device to tailor tone; it is never transmitted.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only data processed off-device is the anonymous purchase transaction above, which contains no personal identifiers.

Third-party services

  • Text-to-speech — the operating system's built-in engine (Apple AVSpeechSynthesizer on iOS) via the open-source flutter_tts package. Runs entirely on the device; no spoken text is sent anywhere.
  • RevenueCat — purchase management, used only for the in-app purchase and restore flow (see "Purchases").

No other third-party SDK is included.

Your rights

Because the app collects no personal information, there is no personal profile to access, correct, or delete. Uninstalling the app removes every locally stored preference. For purchase records, you can manage your purchase history through your Apple/Google account. If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices ever change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before the change ships in a new release.
